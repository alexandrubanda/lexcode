We write like this
Short sentences. Concrete nouns. First person — there is one person here and pretending otherwise is the fastest way to lose trust. Name the deliverable, the timeline, and the price before the adjectives.
We never write like this
“Leveraging cutting-edge synergies to unlock digital transformation.” No “we” that means one guy. No promises with no number in them.
